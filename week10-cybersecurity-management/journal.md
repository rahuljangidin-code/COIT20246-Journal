## Task 2 – NIST Cyber Security Framework Objectives

Four security objectives were selected from the NIST Cyber Security Framework. Two objectives were chosen from the Protect function and two from the Detect function. Each objective is from a different category.

### Selected NIST Objectives

| Function | Category | Sub-category | Reason | Attack / Vulnerability Mitigated |
|--------|----------|--------------|--------|----------------------------------|
| Protect | PR.AC | PR.AC-1 | Ensures that system access is limited to authorised users only. | Prevents unauthorised access and credential misuse attacks. |
| Protect | PR.DS | PR.DS-1 | Protects sensitive data through encryption and access controls. | Reduces risk of data breaches and data leakage. |
| Detect | DE.CM | DE.CM-1 | Enables continuous monitoring of network traffic and systems. | Detects malware activity and abnormal network behaviour. |
| Detect | DE.AE | DE.AE-1 | Allows early identification of security incidents. | Minimises impact of cyber attacks by early detection. |
